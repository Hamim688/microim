## Brand & Style

The design system projects sovereign authority in hardware-software convergence, deep-tech computing, and precision IoT engineering. It combines the industrial restraint of Apple Pro architecture, the computational intensity of NVIDIA, and the aerospace-grade utility of Tesla telemetry systems. The visual stance balances rigorous minimalism with high-frequency technological vitality—eschewing decorative noise in favor of functional luminescence, optical clarity, and absolute geometric confidence.

The design movement is **Precision Cyber-Minimalism**: ultra-deep, mathematically ordered navy surfaces overlaid with translucent glassmorphic planes, micro-etched circuit geometries, and sharp vector accents of warm optical luminescence. It evokes uncompromising precision, operational security, and high-performance intelligence.

## Colors

The palette operates on a high-density tonal architecture engineered for low-light legibility and sustained visual immersion:

- **Primary Canvas & Depth Layers:**
  - Base Obsidian Void: `#050D1A` (Root background canvas, system substrate)
  - Surface Base: `#071426` (Primary container background)
  - Elevated Surface Layer: `#0B1E38` (Interactive cards, floating toolbars, elevated drawers)
  - Surface Highlight Overlay: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Tactile glass overlays)

- **Accents & Circuit Emissives:**
  - Electric Circuit Gold: `#FFC928` (Primary brand action, focused states, interactive emitters, telemetry peaks)
  - Core Luminescence / Hover Amber: `#F59E0B` (Interactive transitions, telemetry warnings, dynamic current nodes)
  - Optical Glow Alpha: `rgba(255, 201, 40, 0.15)` (Ambient edge illumination, selective trace lighting)

- **Typographic & Interface Contrast:**
  - Signal White: `#FFFFFF` (Primary headlines, key metrics, critical telemetry values)
  - Cold Slate: `#94A3B8` (Body prose, default system labels, inactive icons)
  - Deep Slate / Divider: `#334155` (Subtle boundary vectors, quiescent grid traces, inactive controls)
  - Trace Ghost Stroke: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Universal card edge delineation)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional three-engine structure:

1. **Space Grotesk** governs primary titles, section headers, and hero metrics. Its geometric proportions deliver a futuristic, high-spec industrial aesthetic.
2. **Geist** powers systemic interaction copy, narrative overviews, and dense analytical dashboards. It provides clean, ultra-refined horizontal proportioning and neutral legibility at micro scales.
3. **JetBrains Mono** anchors technical readouts, operational states, data keys, hardware node addresses, and tabular metrics. Use uppercase styling with letter-spacing tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) for hardware-calibrated status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine is built upon an 8px atomic grid anchored within a strict 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, shifting to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fluid container capped at 1440px max width; outer canvas margin of `2.5rem` (`margin`), internal column gutters of `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column configuration; margins collapse to `1.5rem`, gutters remain `1rem`.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout; margin scales down to `1rem` (`margin-mobile`), gutter shrinks to `0.75rem` (`gutter-mobile`).

Background grids can feature optional vector trace overlays: ultra-subtle coordinate crosshairs (`+`) at 128px intervals, rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`, reinforcing the architectural schematic environment without cluttering layout flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, fuzzy drop shadows. Instead, it relies on **Layered Glassmorphism**, **Vector Specular Outlines**, and **Emissive Circuit Glows**:

- **Substrate Level (Layer 0):** Deepest background `#050D1A` with faint ambient mesh gradients in deep cobalt.
- **Glassmorphic Paneling (Layer 1):** Solid `#071426` or translucent `rgba(11, 30, 56, 0.65)` surfaces backed by a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` layer. Outlined with a razor-thin 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Raised Interactive Paneling (Layer 2):** `#0B1E38` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` with top-edge specular highlights (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`).
- **Luminescent Accent State (Emissive Elevation):** Triggered on critical data cards, focused inputs, or active hardware alerts. Visualized via a dual border effect: 1px continuous stroke of `#FFC928` paired with an external directional glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(255, 201, 40, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, controlled edge radii (`roundedness: 1`). Structural components rely on a disciplined `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, expanding to `0.5rem` (8px) for large content modules and modal frames. This maintains a sharp, machined hardware chassis feel reminiscent of milled aluminum components and industrial server racks. Full circular pills are strictly forbidden, except for status indicator LEDs and micro-tag pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic:** High-contrast Electric Circuit Gold (`#FFC928`) background with obsidian typography (`#050D1A`), weight 600, uppercase monospace label tracking. On hover: shifts to `#F59E0B` with a soft optical halo (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 201, 40, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Glass:** `rgba(11, 30, 56, 0.8)` background, 1px perimeter trace (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), Signal White text (`#FFFFFF`). On hover: stroke brightens to `#FFC928`, background shifts to `rgba(255, 201, 40, 0.05)`.
- **Hardware Trigger (Ghost):** Fully transparent background, Slate Grey text (`#94A3B8`), subtle inner icon. On hover: Signal White with micro-pulse underline.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Background `#071426`, 1px boundary of `#334155`, text set in `JetBrains Mono` at `#FFFFFF`, placeholder in `#64748B`.
- **Focused State:** Border transitions instantaneously to `#FFC928`, casting an inner trace highlight. A micro circuit node indicator lights up in the top right corner.

### Cards & Modules
- Structured container layered in `rgba(11, 30, 56, 0.5)` with `16px` backdrop blur. Bound by a 1px boundary of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Card headers incorporate an optional corner technical index (`[SYS.01]`, `[IOT.BUS]`) in `JetBrains Mono` label-sm text.

### Chips & Status Nodes
- Compact height (24px), border radius 4px (`roundedness: 1`), background `rgba(7, 20, 38, 0.85)`, stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Features an active hardware pip (6px circle) glowing solid green `#10B981` (nominal), amber `#FFC928` (active bus), or crimson `#EF4444` (interrupted link).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 3px rounded squares for checkboxes, octagonal or minimal squares with center points for radios.
- Inactive: 1px border of `#334155`, interior `#050D1A`.
- Active: Border and check glyph in `#FFC928`, internal background `#0B1E38`.

### Circuit Telemetry Bars (Additional Component)
- Segmented linear status monitors composed of discrete 2px-wide vertical tick blocks.
- Unfilled ticks use `#1E293B`; filled active capacity ticks emit high-intensity `#FFC928` with an alpha falloff on trailing edges.
